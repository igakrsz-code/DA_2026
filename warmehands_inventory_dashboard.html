05 / SMART PURCHASING
Recommendations for WarmeHands Inc.
🟢 Prioritise Class A Stock Replenishment
18 SKUs drive 68.5% of revenue. Never allow stockouts on products like GROW A FLYTRAP TIN (£33.7K), SET OF 6 SOLDIER SKITTLES (£26.2K), or WOODLAND CHARLOTTE BAG (£24K). Maintain safety stock of at least 2× average monthly demand.
🔴 COGS Exceed Revenue — Urgent Pricing Review
Total 2020 COGS (£1.24M) vastly exceeds revenue (£599K). Negative margins are systemic, not isolated. Factory equipment rent and advertisement costs are primary drivers. A cost audit and retail price adjustment is critical.
🟡 Liquidate 39 Slow-Moving SKUs
37% of SKUs have turnover below 5×, tying up capital in excess inventory. Office & School category averages only 3.4× turnover. Consider promotional pricing, bundling, or discontinuing the lowest performers (Class C, TO < 2×).
🔵 Lean Into Q4 Seasonality
October and November together generated £126K — 28% of annual revenue. Purchasing plans should front-load stock by September. Q1 is the weakest period (£17K in January) — minimise orders then.
🟢 Expand UK & USA Market Focus
United Kingdom (£60K) and USA (£51K) are the top two markets and together represent 25% of 2021 revenue. Targeted marketing and local stocking agreements in these markets could significantly increase throughput for Class A products.
🟡 Jewelry Category: High Turnover, Low Volume
Jewelry has the highest average turnover (23.2×) but only £3,967 in 2021 revenue across 13 SKUs. This signals strong demand relative to stock levels — consider significantly increasing purchasing quantities for this category.
